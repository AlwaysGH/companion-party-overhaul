# Companion Party Overhaul

A mod for [Ragnarok Offline](https://github.com/Flux159/ragnarokoffline.app).

Your companions as a real party of people who live in this world with you. This is Companion Life and Companion Tactics in one mod.

**Every feature can be turned on or off** in the mod's Settings window.

## How they fight

- Each class plays its part like a real player, at bosses and in every fight: normal attacks most of the time, SP saved for the bad moments, and spent when it's nearly full.
- Healers heal the right person at the right time, with Kyrie on the tank and Safety Wall on the back line. Casters freeze, burst and interrupt monsters that heal or summon. The front line pulls packs off the back line and falls back to the Priest when overwhelmed.
- They go first for whatever is hitting the healer, then whatever is hitting the casters, then your target.
- You give orders with one click in the window's **Orders** tab, or in party chat: `assist me`, `free fight`, `regroup`, `heal me`, `buff me`, `kyrie me`, `wall me`, `land protector`, `aoe`, `trap`, `take a break` and `let's go`.
- `go hunt` sends them roaming as a group: they follow the tank, wait for each other and gather to revive the fallen. `come back` calls them back.
- **MVP Hunt:** `find the mvp` (or **Find MVP** on the Orders tab) sends them hopping the map with Fly Wings (Acolyte classes Teleport for SP). The one who finds an MVP or a rare mini-boss calls it out, keeps a safe distance and marks it red on your minimap. You answer **Engage together**, **Stand by, I'll solo** or **Retreat**, then Fly Wing straight to them. During a solo, say `help me` in party chat to call everyone in.
- **Stand By:** `stand by` holds them where they are and out of your fight. They only defend themselves.
- Right-click a companion's party frame to Fly Wing to them.
- `take us back` (or `take us home`, `open a portal`): an Acolyte-line companion opens a Warp Portal to your save point, and from town back to the exact spot you left. It costs a Blue Gemstone from their bags or yours (a setting).
- They use the items you give them. They cure bad statuses, use Yggdrasil Seeds and Berries when they're in danger, revive the fallen with Yggdrasil Leaves (whoever carries one runs over), and drink speed potions near a boss. This needs the game's Companion inventory setting.
- **Potions and food like a careful player:** in a fight, or while you walk on, a companion under 50% HP (a setting) uses the healing item from its bag that best fits the wound: potions, herbs, Meat, Cheese... Under 25% the strongest one. A Priest or Acolyte gets 2 seconds to heal first, and a heavy bag never stops them. Standing still after a fight, they sit and rest.
- Hunters lay traps with a purpose: a snare in front of whatever chases the Priest or the casters, Sandman on packs, Claymore on bunched monsters.
- **Assassins dual wield:** give an Assassin a dagger, one-handed sword or axe while their left hand is empty, and it goes in the left hand.
- They don't buff in town when no monster is in sight.

## How they grow

- Stats follow real player builds for each class.
- Their gear refines as they level (+4 at 40, up to +10 at 180), they transcend when you do, and broken gear gets repaired. A Blacksmith or Whitesmith companion also repairs YOUR broken gear for SP (Orders tab, Merchant services).
- A companion that starts with no gear gets one starter kit of shop gear for its class and level.

## How they live

- **Portraits:** when a companion speaks, their class portrait slides in at the bottom right with the line in a speech bubble, like an NPC's. Battle callouts go first, in a red bubble. Every pre-renewal class has one, male and female.
- They talk. They greet you, react to places, call out in battle and answer you in party chat. Hundreds of conversations grow from strangers to close friends.
- **Life stories:** every class has its own past, told in five chapters as your bond grows, by the road and around the campfire. When two of them talk quietly, you're asked "Listen in?". A choice you make shapes what comes after.
- **Moods:** conversations can be heartfelt, funny, a little flirty or an argument. Then they sulk, make up and sit together.
- **"Hey boss" moments:** over 100 questions and encounters. They ask what to call you, put votes to you, ask for advice and react to MVPs, nights and towns. Your answers grow the bond. Everything you've heard is kept in their **Journal**.
- **Pop quiz:** now and then a companion tests you on monsters, drops, elements and places (over 4,000 questions). Right answers earn Cash Points, and streaks earn card albums. Got one wrong? They explain the answer, which makes it a fun way for new players to learn the game.
- **Shopping trips:** back in town, one asks "Shall we go shopping?". Say yes and they wander town on their own, then come back with their restock list bought. Or say `go shopping`.
- Campfire nights: after you've stood still for 3 minutes they make camp on their own, or you say `make camp`. They sit around the fire, talk and cook a meal that buffs the party, and you leave Well Rested with bonus EXP.
- Now and then a companion brings you a gift.
- **Pets:** when a companion reaches the Companion bond, it walks over with the little one that has been following it and asks: "Can we keep it?" Say yes and you name it; say "Not now" and it asks once more later. Each class has its own classic pet (a Lunatic for a Priest, a Baby Desert Wolf for an Archer...). Pets fight beside their companion, sleep by the campfire, cheer at MVPs, play with each other, and stay crying beside a fallen companion until they're back up. Switch each pet on or off, or rename it, in the Bag tab.
- **Bonds:** they remember your adventures (time together, fights, MVPs, places, gifts, campfire nights), and that grows your bond with each one. Tiers: Acquaintance, Companion (+2% max HP and SP), Trusted (better gifts, camp meals last longer), Sworn (+4%), Kindred (+6%).
- A Merchant-line companion can **appraise** your unknown items, 10 SP each, like Item Appraisal.
- Between fights they stand loosely around you, look around and walk patrols (scouts). They sit down beside the one they feel closest to.
- A party purse fills with a little zeny from every monster you beat.
- **Party loot:** monsters the party beats sometimes turn up an extra find from their own drop list, into the party bag. Sell it from the Loot tab to fill the purse, or take it yourself.
- **Restock in town:** give each companion a shopping list (potions, Blue Gemstones, arrows...). Whenever you reach a town they top up from the party purse, at shop prices, with a Merchant's Discount. Red and Yellow Gemstones too, if you turn that setting on (NPC shops only sell Blue).
- Fallen companions get back up when you leave the map.
- Their lines in your chat box come in a colour of your choice.

## Companions window

Press **Alt+N**, or use the small on-screen button. Drag its corner to resize it. Four tabs:

- **Orders:** your orders as buttons. Fight stance (Engage, Protect, Hold), Focus on your target, the Task card (Follow, Stand By, Hunt, Find MVP), Make camp / Pack up, Warp Portal and Merchant services (Appraise, Repair). An order nobody can carry out is greyed out, with the reason ("No Acolyte in the party", "No Blue Gemstone"...).
- **Bag:** one companion at a time (pick them by name), laid out like the game's Equip window: their gear around them and the companion drawn in the middle. Below: their bag, a colour-coded weight bar (yellow from 50%, when natural regen stops; red from 90%, when they can't attack), and their **Restock** list.
- **Loot:** the party purse and the party bag. **Sell all** sells everything but cards into the purse.
- **Roster:** everyone you've saved, with portraits, Active and Inactive. Hire (your own class tier, at your level, for a one-time fee; your first companion is free), summon, bench, rebirth, homunculus and part ways. Each companion's ⋯ menu has **Stats** (live HIT, FLEE, DEF, CRIT, ASPD), **Skills**, **Role** and **Bond** (tier, perks and their Journal). The game menu's Companions button opens it (a setting).

**Party frames:** everyone's face, HP and SP on screen all the time, as a top row or a left column, at any size. Frames show bad statuses in the game's own colours, shake on a big hit, glow on a heal or low SP, and flash on a level up. Click one to open that companion's bag.

Drag an item from your inventory onto a companion to give it, or onto its Restock row to add it to the list. For a stack, you pick how many. Gear they can wear goes on at once, including an accessory on each side, even two of the same ring. Hover an item to see it; double-click it, or drag it onto your inventory, to take it back.

## Install

In Ragnarok Offline, open **Mods → Find Mods** and install **companion-party-overhaul**. Updates show up there too.

To install by hand, download the zip from [Releases](../../releases), then install it from the Mods tab.

If Find Mods can't install it, it can't reach GitHub's downloads. That can happen on some networks, or after more than 60 lookups an hour from one network. Download the zip from Releases on that computer and install it from the Mods tab.

## Requirements

- Ragnarok Offline 1.7.8 or newer
- Pre-renewal (recommended) or renewal

On renewal the mod loads and the talk, memories, campfires, gifts and window work the same. Builds, starter kits, refine levels and the party roles follow pre-renewal rules, and the 3rd classes have no tactics of their own yet.

## Settings

You can turn every feature on or off in the mod's Settings window: party spacing (Close, Normal, Far), party roles, roaming, campfires, gifts, growth, sitting, chat and its colour, emotes, the purse, party loot (Off, Low, Normal, Generous), portraits (size, reading speed, small talk or callouts only), the Warp Portal's gem, memories and bonds, "Hey boss" moments, the pop quiz, shopping trips, party frames, pets and how hard they hit, and more.
