# Companion Party Overhaul

A mod for [Ragnarok Offline](https://github.com/Flux159/ragnarokoffline.app).

Your companions as a real party of people who live in this world with you. This is Companion Life and Companion Tactics in one mod.

## How they fight

- Each class plays its part, at bosses and in every fight. The front line protects you, the back line keeps its distance, casters save area spells for groups, and Sages endow weapons.
- You give orders in party chat, such as `assist me`, `free fight`, `regroup`, `heal me`, `buff me`, `kyrie me`, `wall me`, `land protector`, `aoe`, `trap`, `take a break` and `let's go`.
- They use the items you give them. They cure bad statuses, use Yggdrasil Seeds and Berries when they're in danger, and drink speed potions near a boss. This needs the game's Companion inventory setting.
- They don't buff in town when no monster is in sight.

## How they live

- They talk. They greet you, react to places, call out in battle and answer you in party chat. About 200 conversations grow from strangers to close friends.
- They remember your adventures: where you met, the bosses you beat together and the places you saw.
- Between fights they stand loosely around you, look around and walk patrols (scouts). They sit down beside the one they feel closest to.
- A party purse fills with a little zeny from every monster you beat.
- Companions spend their own stat points. Fallen companions get back up when you leave the map.

## Companions window

Press **Alt+N**, or use the small on-screen button. The window shows everyone's HP, SP, level and state, plus their memories, bags, the gear you gave, their colours and the party purse.

In the **Bag** tab, drag an item from your inventory onto a companion to give it. For a stack, you pick how many. Gear they can wear goes on at once. Click an item to see it; double-click to take it back.

## Install

In Ragnarok Offline, open **Mods → Find Mods** and install **companion-party-overhaul**. Updates show up there too.

To install by hand, download the zip from [Releases](../../releases), then install it from the Mods tab.

## Requirements

- Ragnarok Offline 1.5.9 or newer
- Pre-renewal or renewal

## Settings

You can turn every feature on or off in the mod's Settings window. That includes party spacing (Close, Normal, Far), sitting, patrols, chat, emotes, the purse, memories and more.
